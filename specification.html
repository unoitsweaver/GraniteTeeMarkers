Specification

Blocks

Blocks are naturally formed ‘split stone’ millions of years old.

The sizes are approximately 100mm × 100mm × 100mm. Sizes vary since this is a totally natural product. Any size variation only adds to the charm of the product.

Paint

The paint is formulated for strong adhesion to the granite, and extreme weather durability.

(Due to the uneven texture of block they must not be stacked upon each other.)

Colours

Standard colours are:

  • Pure WhiteRAL 9010
  • Signal YellowRAL 1003
  • Ruby RedRAL 3003orTomato RedRAL 3013
  • Regatta BlueBS 4800
